The before-45 list

Pick one, or pick a few. Each is rated by how much it asks of you, from 1 (easy) to 4 (epic). Open any card for the details.

    Put a stake in the ground

    This year ends when I turn 45. The people don't. So here's the real ask: pick something we'd both happily do again, name a date, and keep it.

    1. Year one We figure it out. Where, what to eat, who drives.
    2. Year two We already know the plan.
    3. Year three Nobody has to ask what to bring.
    4. Year four You text me before I text you.

    That's how a friendship gets a shape. Not one big trip we talk about forever. The same good thing, on the same weekend, with the same faces, every year. No outdoing last time. No new theme. Just showing up again.

    The easy cards make the best traditions. A dinner I'll happily repeat beats an epic I'll dread. When you send a request, tell me if you want it to be the first of many. If you do, the calendar invite repeats every year.
